# Gym app on your Android phone

The app keeps everything on your phone. You do not need an account.

## 1. Install it

1. On your phone, open **Chrome** (not Samsung Internet).
2. Go to **https://iamvikrant97.github.io/gym-tracker/**
3. Tap the **three dots** (top right), then **Add to Home screen** (on some phones: **Install app**), then **Install**.
4. Open **Gym** from your home screen from now on.
5. Open it once with internet before your first gym visit. After that it works without signal.

## 2. First-time setup (1 minute)

The app asks four short things:

1. **How many days a week?** Tap 2, 3, 4 or 5, then tap the days you want. Monday, Wednesday and Friday is the default for 3.
2. **Choose your plan.** "Full body A/B" is suggested for 2 or 3 days. Or pick "Build my own" to name your sessions and choose your exercises.
3. **Your week.** Check the days and sessions.
4. Tap **Start**.

Start light for the first 2 weeks and stop 2 to 3 reps before you cannot do any more.
You can change the plan any time in **Settings** (gear on the Today screen) under **Training plan**. Your history stays.

## 3. Doing a session

1. On **Today**, tap **Start workout**.
2. On the first set of an exercise, tap the tick. The app asks for the weight: tap **+** until it is right (hold to go faster), then **Save set**.
3. For the next sets, one tap on the tick logs the same weight and reps.
4. The rest timer starts by itself. Tap **Finish workout** at the end.

Next time, the app suggests the weight and reps for you.

## 4. Music with Spotify (optional)

You need your own **Spotify Premium**.

1. Send Vik the email you use for Spotify. He adds it in the Spotify developer dashboard. Until he does, login will not work.
2. In the app open **Settings**, then **Spotify**.
3. Paste the **Client ID** (ask Vik) and tap **Connect Spotify**.
4. Log in and tap **Agree**. You come back to the app.

Open the Spotify app and play any song first, then control it from the gym app.

## 5. Back up your history

Your history is only on this phone. If you clear Chrome data or uninstall the app, it is gone.

Every few weeks: **Settings**, scroll to **Backup**, tap **Export backup**. A file is saved in Downloads. Keep it somewhere safe, like Google Drive or WhatsApp to yourself. To restore, use **Import backup file**.
